VAR_INPUT
B_EN | Bit | (Cho phep chay FB, M500 ON moi xu ly day lay)
bPush | Bit | (Xung lenh day 1 phoi moi, bat canh len)
bPop | Bit | (Xung lenh lay 1 phoi ra, bat canh len)
rPushX | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (X tam day vao mm)
rPushY | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Y tam day vao mm)
rPushAng | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Goc xoay day vao radian)
rPushE0 | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Moc E day vao mm)

VAR_OUTPUT
bOk | Bit | (Bao 1 thao tac day hoac lay vua thanh cong)
bFull | Bit | (Bao hang day 16 phoi, chan day them)
bEmpty | Bit | (Bao hang rong 0 phoi, khong lay duoc)
rPopX | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (X tam vua lay ra mm)
rPopY | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Y tam vua lay ra mm)
rPopAng | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Goc vua lay ra radian)
rPopE0 | FLOAT (Single Precision) | (Moc E vua lay ra mm)
iCnt | Word[Signed] | (So phoi dang cho 0..16)

VAR
qX | FLOAT (Single Precision)(0..15) | (Mang FIFO 16 o X mm)
qY | FLOAT (Single Precision)(0..15) | (Mang FIFO 16 o Y mm)
qAng | FLOAT (Single Precision)(0..15) | (Mang FIFO 16 o goc radian)
qE | FLOAT (Single Precision)(0..15) | (Mang FIFO 16 o moc E mm)
iHead | Word[Signed] | (Chi so dau hang 0..15)
iTail | Word[Signed] | (Chi so duoi hang 0..15)
bPrevPush | Bit | (Nho canh len cua bPush)
bPrevPop | Bit | (Nho canh len cua bPop)
